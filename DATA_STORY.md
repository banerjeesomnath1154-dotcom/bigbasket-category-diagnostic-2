# 📊 BigBasket Executive Data Story & Category Recommendations

## 1. Executive Summary & Macro Revenue Trajectory
During the first half of 2026 (January through June), BigBasket generated **₹88,282** in total revenue across **434 delivered customer orders**, achieving an overall platform Average Order Value (AOV) of **₹203.41**.

Monthly revenue demonstrated strong growth from January (**₹11,057**) through May (**₹17,231**), representing a peak month-over-month expansion of **+55.8%**. This growth was driven by increasing customer order frequency across household and fresh grocery categories. However, June experienced a contraction down to **₹13,608 (-21.0% MoM)**, signaling seasonal demand softening or promotional withdrawal that requires category-level intervention.

---

## 2. Category Revenue Performance vs Target Tiers

BigBasket's category management team established a combined monthly revenue benchmark of **₹86,000** across 6 core product categories. While total revenue exceeded the overall platform benchmark by **+2.65% (+₹2,282)**, diagnostic performance varies significantly across categories:

### 🟢 Above Target (Anchor Revenue Drivers - 3 Categories)
1. **Household Essentials**: Delivered **₹21,715** against a ₹17,000 target (**+27.74% variance / +₹4,715**). High price-point items (cleaning supplies, detergents) and strong order volume (79 orders) established this as BigBasket's #1 revenue category.
2. **Bakery**: Delivered **₹15,410** against a ₹12,000 target (**+28.42% variance / +₹3,410**). High repeat purchasing frequency (67 orders) powered steady target overachievement.
3. **Personal Care**: Delivered **₹16,382** against a ₹15,500 target (**+5.69% variance / +₹882**). High average basket value (₹248.21) maintained positive margin contributions.

### 🟡 Below Target - Watch (Moderate Deficit - 1 Category)
4. **Dairy & Eggs**: Generated **₹14,090** against a ₹16,500 target (**-14.61% shortfall / -₹2,410**). Order volume was robust (66 orders), but lower basket price points kept the category slightly below target.

### 🔴 Below Target - Critical (Severe Revenue Shortfall - 2 Categories)
5. **Fruits & Vegetables**: Generated **₹9,790** against a ₹12,000 target (**-18.42% shortfall / -₹2,210**). Ranking last in total revenue despite 73 orders, this category suffers from low order value (₹134.11 AOV) and perishable inventory loss.
6. **Snacks & Beverages**: Generated **₹10,895** against a ₹13,000 target (**-16.19% shortfall / -₹2,105**). Despite recording the highest order volume on the platform (**83 orders**), low product unit prices produced the lowest AOV on the platform (**₹131.27**), severely bottlenecking revenue conversion.

---

## 💡 Actionable Category Management Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Catalog Restructuring & Multi-Pack Bundling for Snacks & Beverages
* **Diagnostic Finding**: Snacks & Beverages achieves the highest customer order traffic (83 orders) but the lowest average order value (₹131.27).
* **Strategic Action**: Category managers should phase out single-unit SKU listings at checkout and mandate 3-pack/5-pack combo listings (e.g. "Family Snack Packs" or "Beverage Bundles"). Raising the minimum purchase threshold will increase Snacks & Beverages AOV above ₹180 without sacrificing order volume, directly closing the ₹2,105 target gap.

### Recommendation 2: Supplier Review & Fresh Subscription Model for Fruits & Vegetables
* **Diagnostic Finding**: Fruits & Vegetables suffers from a severe 18.42% revenue shortfall (₹9,790 vs ₹12,000 target) and low basket sizes.
* **Strategic Action**: Audit supply chain partnerships with **FreshFarms Co** and **GreenValley Traders** to eliminate stock-outs on high-margin produce (e.g. premium organic fruits). Concurrently, launch a weekly "Fresh Produce Box" automated subscription program for repeat households to lock in recurring monthly revenue.
